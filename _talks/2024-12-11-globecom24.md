针对线性阵列在近场定位中边缘分辨率崩塌、距离估计误差大的问题，介绍了分扇区均匀环形阵列（sUCA）的设计，并给出基于波前反向传播模拟的低复杂度近场定位方法及其性能分析。

This talk introduces the sectored uniform circular array (sUCA) design for near-field localization, which mitigates the edge-resolution collapse and large ranging errors of linear arrays, and presents a low-complexity localization method based on wavefront back-propagation emulation together with its performance analysis.
